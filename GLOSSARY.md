# Sejmomat

Language for reading parliamentary records and showing them alongside a broadcast.

## Broadcasts

**Picture time**: The moment in the parliamentary recording currently visible to the viewer, which can differ from wall-clock time and the time at which a caption is recognised.
_Avoid_: live time, recognition time

**Broadcast availability**: Whether a broadcast is confirmed to contain playable video, is only scheduled, is still ahead, or cannot currently be checked. A scheduled start alone does not confirm a live broadcast.
_Avoid_: live status inferred from the timetable

**Ready MP photo**: A decoded portrait available for immediate use in a parliamentary graphic. A known portrait address or a planned download does not make a photo ready.
_Avoid_: available photo when only its address is known
