Club logos that replace the Sejm API's own (`clubs/{id}/logo`) on the stream's cards
(tools/live_overlay.py, `club_logo`), where that one does not look right on a card.
A file here is named after the club's id, e.g. `PiS.png`; PNG with transparency.

- `PiS.png`: "Logo of the Law and Justice.svg" from English Wikipedia
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Logo_of_the_Law_and_Justice.svg), public domain
  (below the threshold of originality), a registered trademark of Prawo i Sprawiedliwość;
  rendered at 960 px by Wikimedia and scaled to 256 px. The Sejm API's version is a small
  JPEG whose white background shows as a box on the card.
